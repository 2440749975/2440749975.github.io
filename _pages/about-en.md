I will receive my Ph.D. degree from the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, Hubei, China. My supervisor is Professor Yihua Kang. I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Manufacturing Science and Engineering (now School of Mechanical Engineering), Sichuan University, Chengdu, Sichuan, China. Including co-authored papers, I have published 20+ academic papers
 <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WMkMTb4AAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url={{ url | url_encode }}&logo=Google%20Scholar&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a>.

My research interests include:
- Instrumentation
- Mechanical Engineering
- Electromagnetic Nondestructive Testing
- Magnetic Flux Leakage Testing
- Eddy Current Testing
- Magnetic Sensors
- NDT Equipment Development
